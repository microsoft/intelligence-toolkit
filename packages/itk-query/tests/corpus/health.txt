Public health agencies warn that heat waves increase hospital admissions. Hospital capacity is strained during heat waves, especially for elderly patients. Air pollution worsens respiratory illness during heat waves.

Vaccine programs remain central to public health. Vaccine programs reduce hospital admissions and protect hospital capacity during winter outbreaks. Public health budgets fund vaccine programs and air quality monitoring.

Air pollution from carbon emissions causes respiratory illness. Respiratory illness rates are highest in dense urban areas with poor air quality monitoring. Public health researchers study air pollution and respiratory illness together.
